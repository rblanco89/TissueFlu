Notes from the simcovcuda code:
- The cells have a poisson distrubuted incubation, expression, apoptosis period (in this moment I have identical cells)
- Aparently, each cell accumulate virions and inflamation of their neighbors (cells in contact) in each step.
- The cell increase the virions according to the difference of the actual virion level times the diffusion parameter with the actual virion level plus the avetage of the diffused virion of its neighborhood, minus the clearence rate:
		virionsDiffused = v->virions*opt.virionDiffusion;
        virionsLeft = v->virions - virionsDiffused;
        avgNBVirions = (virionsDiffused + v->nbVirions*opt.virionDiffusion)/(v->neighborhoodSize);

        v->virions = virionsLeft + avgNBVirions;

        v->virions = (1.0 - opt.virionClearance)*v->virions;
- The inflamation has the same process.
- A healthy cell is infected if the level of its virions times infectivity parameter meet a succesful Bernoulli trial.
- The number of virions increase a certain quantity (virion production parameter) each time step, idem for inflamation.
